## 1. The core legal reality (read first)

The website and paid ads are your **highest-risk surfaces**. Three hard rules:

1. **Never use images grabbed from Google Images.** They're copyrighted by the photographer/brand. Using them commercially = infringement. "It was on Google" is not a license.
2. **Never republish a competitor's actual ad / photoshoot creative** on your site or paid ads. Their studio photo is their copyright. The side-by-side "their ad vs our ad" with *their real image* is the riskiest thing on the list — avoid it on commercial surfaces.
3. **Don't name a competitor and claim superiority** ("better than [Brand]'s shoot") on paid/website. That's disparagement + comparative-advertising territory (active case law in India).

These don't kill the content idea — they just shape *how* you do it (below).

---

## 2. Where to legally get the "raw" input photo

You feed Autmn a plain product photo (the "before"). Legal sources, best first:

- ✅ **Shoot it yourself** — products you buy (the store-bought sweets/items). You own the physical item; your photo is 100% yours. **This is the best path and what tomorrow's store run is for.**
- ✅ **CC0 / public-domain stock**: Unsplash, Pexels, Pixabay. Free for commercial use. Prefer **unbranded/generic** items (a plain serum bottle, a candle, a sweet box) — and avoid images that prominently show another brand's logo or a recognizable person's face.
- ✅ **Properly licensed stock**: Adobe Stock, Shutterstock, Getty — with a commercial license you actually buy. Keep the receipt/license.
- ❌ Google Images / Pinterest / a brand's website / a competitor's ad — not licensed, not safe.

## 3. The comparison content — safe framings

Your instinct (AI ad vs real photoshoot) is great. Here's how to land it without legal exposure, by surface:

### Website hero + gallery + paid ads (zero exposure only)
- Use **your own-shot or CC0 unbranded** products.
- Show **before → after**: the plain phone-style photo → the Autmn ad.
- The "vs a studio shoot" punch is **described, not shown with their image**: e.g. *"A studio shoot for this costs ₹10–15k and takes days. This took 2 minutes and ₹49."* — a claim about *you*, no competitor named, no competitor image.

### Organic social (Instagram/reels) — branded comparison allowed, carefully
- You *may* name a brand in **honest, non-disparaging** review framing ("we ran a [Brand] product through Autmn").
- Show **your** before → **your** Autmn after. **Do not paste their ad image.** If you want the "studio look" reference, recreate a generic version yourself or just describe it.
- Truthful, factual, no "ours is better than theirs." Puffery about yourself ("studio-quality in 2 min") is fine.
- Don't make their logo the focal element; don't imply endorsement/partnership.

### Best content of all
- **Real friendly-seller before/afters** (from Phase 6 onboarding). Authentic, fully yours to use, and per the launch doc these convert best. Prioritize collecting these.

## 4. WhatsApp screenshots — use them, selectively

Screenshots of real WhatsApp threads are the most credible proof of the core promise (*no app, works on the phone you already use*). Use them for the "how easy" and "look what I got" moments — not walls of text.

**Do:**
- Show a tight 3–4 bubble flow: send photo → "making your ads…" → finished ad arrives.
- Pair each chat screenshot with the clean output ad beside it.
- Use **Hindi/Hinglish** threads for the Indian audience.
- Stage a **clean demo thread** to screenshot (fresh conversation, no clutter).
- A short **screen-recording** of one real order (photo → ad) beats statics for reels — shows the speed.

**Must scrub before posting:**
- **Blur/remove all phone numbers** — your personal (+1 540…) *and* the business number. Publishing the live business number invites spam/abuse to that line.
- The current screenshots show the **US test number (+1 555 174-8732)** — off-message for Indian sellers. Blur it or wait for the real production number.
- **Don't screenshot known-broken UX** (duplicate style-picker messages, English-fallback bug). A screenshot freezes a bug forever.
- No payment/UPI details.

**Placement:** polished ad outputs → website hero/gallery; curated chat screenshots + screen-recording → "how it works" section + reels.

## 6. Quick decision rules (cheat sheet)

- Posting on **website/paid ad**? → own-shot or CC0 unbranded only. No brand names, no competitor images. ✅
- Posting on **organic social**? → may name a brand in honest, non-disparaging review; still your own before→after, never their ad image. ⚠️
- Found a great image on **Google**? → don't use it. Find it on Unsplash/Pexels or buy a license or shoot it yourself. ❌
- Want the **"vs ₹15k studio shoot"** angle? → describe it as a claim about you; don't show their copyrighted ad. ✅
